## Data and map credits

These are services the app talks to, not software it contains. Their terms and what each receives are in
[docs/data-sources.md](docs/data-sources.md).

- Forecast and air quality: [Open-Meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/), CC BY 4.0. Air quality data is from the Copernicus
  Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS).
- Weather alerts: the US National Weather Service. This app is not endorsed by or affiliated with NOAA or the NWS.
- Tides: NOAA CO-OPS.
- Radar: Weather data by [RainViewer](https://www.rainviewer.com/).
- Street map: © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors.
