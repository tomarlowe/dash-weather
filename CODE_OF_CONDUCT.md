# Code of conduct

Dash Weather is a small, friendly project. This page says how we expect people to treat each other in its issues, pull
requests and any other space run for the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. People here have very different experience, and some are trying a Car Thing app for the first time.
- Assume good intent, and say clearly what you mean. Disagree with an idea, not with a person.
- Stay on topic. Keep issues about the app, and keep what you share about your own situation to what is useful.
- Respect privacy. Do not post anyone's exact location, contact details or other personal information, including your own
  home address in a bug report.
- Give credit where it is due, and say so when a contribution was written with an AI tool.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or demeaning comments, about anyone, for any reason.
- Sexual or violent language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, advertising and anything unrelated to the project.
- Encouraging people to use the app in a way that is unsafe, such as reading it closely while driving.

## When something goes wrong

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people who break these
expectations. This applies to everyone, including the maintainer.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, or open an issue titled "Conduct: please contact
me" without details, and the maintainer will find a private way to talk. Reports are treated privately as far as possible.

This is the project's own short statement, written in plain words. It is not a copy of another project's text.
