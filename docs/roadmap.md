# Roadmap

These are ideas, not promises. Thomas decides what gets built and when. Nothing here is scheduled.

## Next

- **Try 0.2.0 on a real Car Thing** and fix what the [device checklist](device-test-checklist.md) turns up. The dial,
  brightness and standby behavior are the likeliest places for surprises.
- **Real screenshots** of 0.2.0 from a real device to replace the simulated ones.

## Ideas

- A user-set dim level instead of a fixed 30 percent.
- Weather alerts outside the United States, if a free service with suitable terms and no key exists.
- A better source for rain in the next hour outside North America and central Europe.
- Keeping map squares between runs, within the map services' usage rules, so the map appears faster.
- A built-in test screen with very large text for device checks, so a result can be read at a glance.
- More zoom range for the radar, if the radar service ever allows it.
- More than two places.

## Known limits that will not change soon

- Tides cover the United States and its territories only, because that is what NOAA covers.
- Radar stops at zoom 7.
- Alerts can be late or missing, and the app is not for navigation or safety.
